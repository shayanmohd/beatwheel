Beatwheel Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Beatwheel is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. Beatwheel collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. The instrument profiles, temperaments and drift sessions you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, are never uploaded, and are removed when you uninstall the app. They leave the device only when you export a file or share one yourself.

Permissions

Beatwheel requests the following permissions:

  • RECORD_AUDIO (microphone): used only while the tuner is on screen, to measure the pitch of your instrument. The sound is analysed in memory and discarded. No audio is ever saved or sent.

Third parties

Beatwheel contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to SocialSure Private Limited through the developer contact details on the Google Play listing.

There is no server component.
